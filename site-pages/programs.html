The programs behind the house

Lux is a young house, and young houses run on programs — startup credits, founder perks, and our own programs for the people who arrive early. This page keeps them straight, in plain words: what's in hand, what's applied for, and what's still being decided. Those are three different things, and we never blur them.

Our own programs

Programs the house runs for its people

House program · open now

First Access

The house's first releases carry a First Access door: the very first single — A Million Times Better, bundled with its music video and cover — is available now, ahead of the December 1 opening, from the music store. First through the door is a real place in this house's story, and it's remembered.

House program · opens December 1

The founding door

When the house opens, early customers and subscribers come in as founders of their rooms — the research briefings' founding cohort among them. Founding terms are being set carefully and will be printed here, exactly, before anyone is asked to join.

House promise · always on

The Gratitude Ledger

Everyone who helps Lux gets written down, and helpers are repaid first — ahead of any new spending that can wait. It's the house's standing order, and bringing a friend is one of the ways a name gets into that book.

Why we print it this way

Applied is not granted. Filed is not funded.

Plenty of young companies dress up applications as achievements. We'd rather show you the real shelf: one credit truly in hand, a handful of honest applications out in the world, and programs of our own that we control completely. When any status above changes, this page changes with it — same day.
