How we vet — the house rule

Our margin is ours the moment a sale clears

Every program on this shelf is checked the same way before it is listed: How long do they hold our money? What is the minimum before they pay? Can they claw a payment back — and for how long? Long holds, high minimums and wide clawback rights are marks against a program, and a deal that holds our earnings at a platform's sole discretion never makes the shelf. The terms below were checked on each program's own pages by our scouts in November 2026 — and every link on this page goes live only when its program signup is finished. Until then, each slot honestly says so.
