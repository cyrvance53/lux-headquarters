How a project runs

Quote, pay, then we build

  1. Talk to the desk. We agree exactly what's being made — and the price — in writing. Custom and off-catalog work is quoted per project, never guessed from a menu.
  2. You get a payment link made for your project alone, named for you and your job. Small fixed items are paid in full up front; bigger projects run 50% down to start.
  3. Work starts when your payment lands and verifies under your name — not before, never on a promise. That protects you as much as us: a paid booking is a real booking.
  4. You see the finished work. The balance is paid, and then the final files are released. Our margin is ours the moment your sale clears — nobody holds your project, or our earnings, hostage on a timetable.
