How it works

Three steps, all of them gentle

Step one

Tell us who

Once you're a customer, the desk gives you your own referral way in — a personal link and a simple form. Share it however you like: a text, an email, over coffee.

Step two

They arrive as your guest

Your friend lands at Aimée's desk as a welcomed guest, not a cold lead. No spam, no chase — one warm hello, and the rest is their choice.

Step three

You are thanked first

When your friend becomes a customer, both of you are thanked — and your name goes in the house book of people who helped us grow.

The Gratitude Ledger

Helpers are thanked first — always

This house keeps a Gratitude Ledger: everyone who helps Lux gets written down, and when Lux can repay, the people in that book are helped first — ahead of any new spending that can wait.

That is not a marketing line; it is the order of business. Bringing a friend is one of the truest helps there is, and it is recorded like one.

The house list

Hear the house news — your choice, only

Our email list is opt-in only. Names on it arrived because someone asked to be there. We never buy a list, and we never sell or trade this one — not for money, not for favour, not ever. Leaving is one tap, whenever you like.
