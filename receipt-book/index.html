AI Receipt Book

Every action your AI takes, written in a book nobody can quietly rewrite.

The fear is real

When your AI gets it wrong, can you prove what happened?

A company has already been held liable for its own chatbot's bad advice — Air Canada argued the chatbot was a separate entity, and the tribunal disagreed. Most businesses cannot show exactly what their AI did, said, or was told: logs are scattered, editable, or missing, and an editable log is weak evidence.

The relief

A book of receipts that stands up

Every action gets a signed, tamper-evident receipt, written into a hash-chained book at the moment it happens. When anyone asks what happened, you hand them the book.

The product

How AI Receipt Book works

  • A receipt for every action. Signed as it happens, naming the agent and its mandate.
  • Hash-chained and append-only. A quietly changed page breaks the chain — in the open.
  • One readable report. The whole story, exported in a form a non-technical reader can follow.
  • Built for arriving law. The EU AI Act already writes logging and traceability duties into law for covered systems.

One engine runs under every door on this shelf: Amos checks who is knocking. SeamGuard checks the joins between systems. And every check leaves a signed receipt.
