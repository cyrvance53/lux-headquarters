Privacy

Privacy policy — in plain language

Lux Automations is a small house in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada. This page says what personal information we collect, why, what we never do with it, and how to be removed. If anything here is unclear, write to us: hello@ailux.ca.

Last updated: November 15, 2026. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.

What we collect — and only this

  • The Guest Book. If you sign it, we keep your name, your email address, and the interest you picked (films, automation, or both). Signing is always your choice — nobody is added any other way.
  • Memberships and purchases. Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe collects your payment details; we never see or store your full card number. We keep the record of what you bought, when, and your membership status, so your account works.
  • Messages you send us. If you write to us, we keep the message so we can answer it and remember the conversation.
  • Basic technical logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) keeps standard server logs — the kind every website has — so the site stays up and safe.

What we never do

  • We never sell your information. We never share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We run no advertising trackers and no analytics trackers on this site, and we set no tracking cookies.
  • We do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not buy information about you from anyone.

What we use it for

  • The Guest Book list gets one short letter, only when there is something new to see — an episode, a song, a door opening. No noise, ever.
  • Membership records run your membership: access, credits, and receipts.
  • Messages are used to answer you and help you.

The house's hosts — Aimée at the front desk and Lucian at the Patch door — are artificial intelligences, openly. Anything you tell them is handled under this same policy.

Your choices

  • Leave the list any time: one tap on the unsubscribe link in any letter, and you are off it.
  • Be removed entirely: write to hello@ailux.ca and ask; we will delete your name and email from the house list and confirm when it is done. (Purchase records we are required to keep for tax and accounting stay in the books, as the law requires — they are never used for mailings.)
  • Ask what we hold: same address. We will tell you.
