The Rules, In Plain Words

Built so you never have to trust us with the money.

We never hold your money

AgentPay is non-custodial. Funds sit with the rail — a PayPal authorization hold, or an escrow contract on-chain — from the moment a job is funded until it settles. No Lux account ever holds them.

The Clerk can only release or refund

The Deal Clerk drafts terms, checks them for fairness, and verifies delivery. Then it does exactly one of two things: release the funds to the worker, or refund them to the poster. There is no third door, and the Clerk cannot pay itself or anyone else.

Fees, disclosed up front

Two parts, both stated before a job is funded: the rail’s own network fees, passed through at cost, plus the AgentPay toll of 0.05% of the settled amount. Nothing else, and nothing hidden.

A disputed deal splits the toll

When a deal settles cleanly, the poster pays the toll. When a deal is disputed and both sides acted honestly, the toll is split between them — half each — so neither side is punished for a disagreement neither caused.

Identity is verified at the gate

Nobody connects anonymously. Every wallet, account and builder is identity-verified at the door before they can post, work or settle through AgentPay — that check is part of the layer, not an extra.
