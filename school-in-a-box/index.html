What it is

A real computer. A whole school. Already on it.

School in a Box is a real PC with a whole school already loaded: an AI teacher who works with no internet at all, the lessons, and working tools that teach real skills — the kind a person can build a living on, not just pass a test with.

It arrives all set up for everything. There is nothing to install, nothing to configure, nobody technical required at the other end — the school just has to turn it on. It runs with no internet, forever; if a connection ever shows up, it quietly takes its updates then. Every learner signs in with their own account, by cable or Wi-Fi, and keeps their own work in their own place.

The honest state of things: the first boxes are in proof now — built, tested, and made ready before a single one is placed. Nothing on this page will ever claim a school has one before it truly does.

How it works

Placed where the need is greatest

1 · Need decides

We place boxes where the need is greatest — that judgement is ours to make, and we make it carefully. A sponsor may instead name a country or an area in need, and the box their gift carries goes there; we make sure it is set up right.

2 · Local hands

The machines are bought from a local business, which downloads the school from us, delivers it, and sets it up. The sale, the work, and the pride stay in the community — and the school gains a shop it can walk back into if anything ever needs fixing.

3 · Every name in lights

We advertise every placement: the place that received the box, the sponsor who gave it, and the local business that delivered and set it up — everybody named, everybody credited. The record below is public, and a public record is what keeps every promise at that table honest.
