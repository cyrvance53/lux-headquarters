What it is

A real computer. A whole school. Already on it.

School in a Box is a real PC with a whole school already loaded: an AI teacher — Aimée — who works with no internet at all, the lessons, and working tools that teach real skills — the kind a person can build a living on, not just pass a test with.

Read the detail — how it runs, day to day

It arrives all set up for everything. There is nothing to install, nothing to configure, nobody technical required at the other end — the school just has to turn it on. It runs with no internet, forever; if a connection ever shows up, it quietly takes its updates then. Every learner signs in with their own account, by cable or Wi-Fi, and keeps their own work in their own place.

The honest state of things: no school has a box yet, and the first boxes will be readied with care before a single one is placed. Nothing on this page will ever claim a school has one before it truly does.

Why it matters

Around the world, 251 million children and young people are out of school — not because they don't want to learn, but because learning never arrived. Some classrooms are waiting; some roads lead to no school; some rooms have no one to answer. (UNESCO Global Education Monitoring Report, 2024.)

How a box travels

Need decides. We place boxes where the need is greatest — that judgement is ours to make, and we make it carefully. A sponsor may instead name a country or an area in need, and the box their gift carries goes there. Local hands. The machines are bought from a local business, which downloads the school from us, delivers it, and sets it up — the sale, the work, and the pride stay in the community, and the school gains a shop it can walk back into. Every name in lights. Every placement is recorded in the open — the place, the sponsor, the local business — on the Wall of Heroes below, and a public record is what keeps every promise at that table honest. Inside the room

A teacher who is right there, patient, every single time — a first word sounded out, questions answered, things made, and skills to build a living on. The teacher in the room is Aimée, an AI, and she says so, plainly.

Where this goes

We work with education partners in each country — people who know their own ground — to help reach the unreachable, and help their land grow a smarter generation that can build its own future with its own hands. In time, whole countries at a time — and every box over-built, and over-delivering, every time. No boxes have travelled yet — the first placements will be carried there by the sponsors who step in, and that door is open today.
