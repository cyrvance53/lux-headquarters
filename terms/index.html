Terms

Terms of sale & refunds — fair both ways

We are not trying to sucker anybody. We have things that work, we want you to use them and be happy with them — and we don't want to be run over in the process. So these terms are plain, generous where generosity is honest, and firm only where abuse is real. If anything here is unclear, write to us: hello@ailux.ca.

Last updated: November 15, 2026. Changes are posted here and never apply backwards to a period you have already paid for.

What you can buy here

  • Memberships (Entry and Main) — access to the house's rooms and tools, with a bundle of credits, billed monthly or yearly.
  • Credits — the house's metered units, spent when a tool does work. Your balance is always visible on your account.
  • Songs and store items — digital goods, delivered by download or in your library.
  • The Security Patch — for institutions: identity checks and protected payments at their doors, billed month-to-month plus a meter on the checks their doors run.
  • Sponsorships (Sponsor a Kid, School in a Box) are gifts, not purchases — they are covered by their own section below.

Prices and currency. Prices are shown in US dollars (USD). Canadian buyers are charged the same value in Canadian dollars at checkout. Any tax on a sale is calculated on that sale and shown at checkout where it applies.

Cancelling — easy, on purpose

  • Cancel any subscription any time — from your account, or with one email to us. No phone call, no retention maze, no dark patterns.
  • Cancelling stops the next charge. Your access runs to the end of the period you already paid for.
  • The Patch is month-to-month: cancelling stops future months, and any unused prepaid check balance is refunded.

Refunds — the honest path

  • If we fail to deliver what you bought, we refund it in full. No argument, no forms.
  • Changed your mind: a first membership purchase is refunded in full within 14 days if the service has not been substantially used. Yearly plans: full refund in the first 30 days; after that, a pro-rated refund of the unused whole months for the rest of the first year.
  • Credits: unused purchased credits are refundable within 30 days. Credits already spent paid for computing that actually ran, and are not refundable — your balance is always in front of you, so spending is never a surprise.
  • Songs and downloads: if a file will not play or download, we fix it or refund it.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Write to hello@ailux.ca — a refund from us is faster than a chargeback, and we answer.

The firm lines — where abuse is real

  • Chargebacks. Talk to us first; we make genuine mistakes right. If a chargeback is filed for a service that was actually delivered and used, we may dispute it with the records of the delivery (dates, charges, access) — nothing more than the records.
  • Misuse. Fraud, illegal use, or attacking the service itself: we may end that account without a refund for the misused service. For everything else, we talk first.
  • Set it up right — the manual is part of the deal. The Security Patch and its adapters come with a user manual. Install, configure, and run them the way the manual directs, and keep every bot inside the mandate you set for it. If a bot is refused or revoked because it was set up against the manual or run outside its mandate, that rests with you — and losses from a setup the manual warns against are not ours to cover. This changes nothing about the promise above: if we fail to deliver what we sold, we refund it in full.
  • What the products do — and don't — promise. A membership gives access to the house's rooms and tools as they exist; some rooms stand locked while they are being verified, and the site says so openly where that is the case. The Patch provides identity checks and payment records as described on its page; it does not guarantee that fraud will never occur, and no page of this site promises an outcome we cannot control.
  • Liability. Our liability under any purchase is limited to the fees you paid for the period in which the failure occurred. Nothing in these terms limits any right the law gives you that cannot be limited.

Sponsorships are gifts

Sponsoring a kid's membership or a School in a Box is a gift to the mission, not a purchase: no goods or services are owed in return, placement decisions rest with the house, and — because Lux Automations is a business, not a registered charity — sponsorships are not tax-deductible donations and no tax receipt is issued. If you gave by mistake, write within 14 days and we will return it.
