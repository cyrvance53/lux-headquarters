The deal

20% of the profit — on every payment, for as long as they stay

When a customer comes to us through you and pays, you receive 20% of the profit on that payment. Not once — every time. The monthly payment, the yearly payment, and every renewal after that: as long as your customer keeps paying, you keep earning.

It is not tied to one product. Your link refers people to the site; if they buy Amos, SeamGuard, membership, or any other product sold on ailux.ca, the purchase is marked as yours.

The same deal covers membership, too. Bring a subscriber to the Workshop membership — Basic, Pro, or Business — and you earn the same 20% of the profit on every membership payment they make, for as long as they stay. One relationship, everything we sell on the site. Membership earning begins when membership opens to the public on January 1, 2027 — the member rooms are still being verified, and membership is not on sale yet.

Your own link marks your sales, and every sale is tracked by receipt — the same signed-receipt discipline the products themselves run on. What you earned is a record both sides can check, not a promise.

Stated plainly

The small print, in plain words

  • A share of the profit, not the price. Earnings are 20% of the profit on a payment — not 20% of the sticker price the customer paid.
  • Real, settled payments. You earn on payments that settle and stay settled. A payment that is refunded or charged back earns nothing.
  • Site-wide scope. If a customer arrives through your link and buys any product sold on ailux.ca, that purchase is under your link.
  • Receipts are the record. Every sale under your link is receipt-tracked, and the house’s receipts are the record both sides can check.
  • Your dashboard is yours alone. It opens with your personal key and shows only your own record — your link, your dealt articles, and your numbers exactly as the house’s receipts record them.
  • Applications stay in the house. What you send in the application form is kept on the house’s own list and used only to run the affiliate programme.
  • The products’ terms of sale and refunds live at ailux.ca/terms.
