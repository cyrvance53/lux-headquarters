Start a Project

Agree the work with the desk, get your quote, pay — then we build. Money first, then work: that's the house rule, and it protects you too.

How a custom project runs

  1. Talk to the desk — we agree exactly what's being made, and the price, in writing.
  2. You get a payment link made for your project alone. Bigger projects: 50% down starts the work.
  3. We start the moment your payment shows in our account under your name — not before, never on a promise.
  4. You see the finished work; the balance is paid, and the final files are released to you.

Project balance — before final release

Paying with crypto

Crypto follows the same house law as card payments: 50% down starts the work, and the balance is paid before the final files are released — and work starts only on confirmed receipt, matched to your deal. One honest difference: crypto confirmation is done by hand — we watch the address on-chain rather than getting an automatic notice — so allow a little time, and always send the desk your transaction hash.
