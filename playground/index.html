How every tool page works

Named by the job

Tools are named by the job, never by the engine: “Make a Movie from a Photo,” not a model name.

No blank canvas, ever

Every tool opens with a finished example and a “Make one like this” button. Aimée is right there on the page, and a short explainer video shows what the tool is for — in your own walk of life.

The price is on the button

The price is printed on the run button. Failed runs are free. Credits move only when the machine does the expensive step.

Yours stays yours

Members keep the rights in what they make; the house takes only the licence it needs to host and show what members choose to post. Watching is view-only — buying a title outright is a separate, paid right, and then the real file is yours.

Membership

One family of membership packages, offered at both doors — the Workshop and the Playground carry the same ladder. There are no per-tool subscriptions, ever: one membership unlocks ALL the tools on its tier.

Founding members

Join before the house opens and you are a founder: 30% off, honoured for your whole first year. The founding offer closes when the doors open on January 1 — after that, the regular ladder below is the only way in.

Pick your billing day

Each member picks their own monthly billing day — the 1st to the 28th — so the charge follows your payday. Founders’ renewals follow their picked day from February on.

Look around free first

Anyone can log in and look around the Playground free before paying — walk the rooms, see the tools, see the channel that could be yours. Making and posting unlock with membership. The Cinema stays free to view.

Credits, in plain sight

Membership includes a monthly credit allotment. Spend your credits, you buy more — packs and in-place top-up, with a ledger you can see: every credit in and out. No dark patterns. Credits live inside the house only — never a coin.
