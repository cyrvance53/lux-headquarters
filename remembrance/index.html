Remembrance Day · November 11

Lest We Forget

A short film for every Canadian who served — in every era and every role — and for the ones who never came home.

The words of the film

He was nineteen. A private on a gun crew, writing a letter home to his girlfriend by lantern light, in the mud below Vimy Ridge, feeding the guns while the sky caught fire.

He was not a general. None of them were. They were privates and corporals, gunners and kids from farms and fishing towns, who stood in the worst places on earth so the rest of us would never have to.

From the ridge to the beaches. From Korea's snows to Afghanistan's dust. They kept standing. And those who fell gave their lives so that we could live in freedom, in a country at peace.

Everything we have, they paid for — our homes, our families, our quiet mornings. All they ask is that we remember.

So remember the guns. Remember the ridge. Remember them.

Lest we forget.
